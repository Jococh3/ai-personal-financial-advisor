budget spending: Try the 50/30/20 rule: about 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to savings.
savings spending: Build an emergency fund of 3 to 6 months of expenses before investing extra money.
transportation: If transportation is a top cost, compare transit passes, carpooling, or combining trips.
food hyper: Cooking at home usually costs less than eating out. Plan the week's meals before shopping.
barsandrestaurants: Dining out adds up fast. Set a weekly limit for bars and restaurants.
health: If your employer offers an HSA or FSA, use it to pay health costs with pre-tax money.
wellnessandbeauty: Review wellness and beauty memberships. Ones you rarely use are easy savings.
tech: Wait 48 hours before big tech purchases to avoid impulse buys.
travel hotelservices: Set a trip budget before booking, and compare travel and hotel prices early.
fashion: Set a monthly clothing budget and buy on sale when you can.
sportsandtoys leisure contents: Keep hobby and entertainment spending inside a set monthly fun-money amount.
home otherservices: Compare quotes on big home and service purchases, and keep cash set aside for repairs.
fraud suspicious: Report any charge you don't recognize to your bank right away. The sooner you report, the less you may owe.
fraud suspicious: Turn on transaction alerts in your bank app so you see every charge as it happens.
fraud: Freeze or replace any card that has had fraudulent charges.
